# Camera-ready repository update

This update preserves the original 46,080-comparison release and adds only the
completed analyses used in the revised ICTAI manuscript.

## Added

- Complementary-measure preprocessing-rank analysis with pairwise-complete denominators.
- Validated aggregate construct-response results for six perturbation families.
- Attribution-implementation tables and 4,000 aggregate bootstrap replicates.
- Matched drift-source estimates and clustered-bootstrap contrasts.
- A single supplementary audit script and completion records.

## Deliberately excluded

Architecture generalization, BT MRI, lesion localization, and other unfinished
or dissertation-only experiments are not part of this paper release.

## Interpretation safeguards

Drift-source residuals are not separately identified model-training effects or
causal fractions. EDI measures paired attribution disagreement, not clinical
correctness, localization accuracy, causal faithfulness, or preprocessing superiority.
